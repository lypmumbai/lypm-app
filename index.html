Did you know?

👩‍🏫 For teachers & schools · tap to open

A park is a free classroom. Every park page has a nature log your class can fill in together.

  • Tree census: count and name every tree along one path. Compare two parks.
  • Leaf rubbing: paper, crayons, five different leaves. No picking from living trees.
  • Map my park: draw the gates, paths, benches and shade. Where would you add a tap or a toilet?
  • Bird minute: sit still for one minute and count every bird call you hear.

Tip: check the park's timings and closed day on its page before planning a visit.
