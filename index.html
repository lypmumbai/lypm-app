For teachers

A park is a free classroom
  • Tree census: count and name every tree along one path. Compare two parks.
  • Leaf rubbing: paper, crayons, five different leaves. No picking from living trees.
  • Map my park: draw the gates, paths, benches and shade. Where would you add a tap or a toilet?
  • Bird minute: sit still for one minute and count every bird call you hear.

Class visit tip: check the park's weekly closed day first. Rani Baug shuts on Wednesdays and Sanjay Gandhi National Park on Mondays.

About this prototype

Love Your Parks Mumbai, version 8 prototype. Park details were checked against public and BMC sources in October 2026. Timings and fees change, so parks marked "check before you go" still need confirming with the park or BMC.

Events and groups marked "sample" are examples only. The Steps Festival dates are real.
